Zebrafish neuromast

Specimen: zebrafish (Danio rerio) lateral-line neuromast, 3 dpf; iSIM, Richardson-Lucy
deconvolved, motion aligned.
Markers: she:GFP (nuclei, 488 nm), cldnb:lyn-mScarlet (membranes, 561 nm).
Recording: Jacobo lab, Chan Zuckerberg Biohub San Francisco; Erzberger et al. (2020)
Nature Physics 16:949.

Source: ultrack data release, Chan Zuckerberg Biohub San Francisco
https://public.czbiohub.org/royerlab/ultrack/zebrafish_neuromast.ome.zarr
Dataset: zebrafish_neuromast.ome.zarr, 500 timepoints, 2 channels, five pyramid levels.
Register: Bragantini et al. (2025) Nature Methods, ultrack.

License: the deposit states no license; the recording is held by the Jacobo lab. Crops of the
same recording, two single timepoints of its two channels, are redistributed under CC BY-SA 4.0
by Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.22820799 (Luxar benchmark volumes). Public redistribution of this
volume should be confirmed with the recording lab first.

Derived for Grana from pyramid level 2 of the OME-Zarr
(500 x 2 x 19 x 256 x 256, 0.3 x 0.3 x 1.0 um):
- timepoints 100 to 170 in steps of 2, 36 frames in original order
- 192 x 192 lateral crop, origin x 47, y 18 in level 2 pixels: 57.6 x 57.6 x 19.0 um
- channel order preserved: 0 nuclei (c488), 1 membranes (c561)
- each channel divided by its 99.9th percentile over the kept crop (nuclei 0.6841,
  membranes 0.5412), clipped to [0, 1] and rounded to 8 bit; no gamma, no background
  subtraction, no denoising, no interpolation
- classic TIFF, one strip per plane, deflate with horizontal predictor
- no TimeIncrement, because the deposit documents no acquisition interval
- channel names and the display colours (cyan #00E5FF, magenta #FF2D95) are metadata; the
  source declares no channel markup

To recover source intensities, multiply a stored value by its channel percentile and divide by
255.
